# Transverse-momentum distribution

## Abstract

We compute the NNLO correction to $d\sigma/dp_T$ at
$\sqrt{s}=13.6,\mathrm{TeV}$.

## Methods

The top-quark mass is fixed to $m_t = 172.5,\mathrm{GeV}$.
We use an integrated luminosity of $35\,\mathrm{fb}^{-1}$.
With $\mu_R$ the renormalization scale, we vary $\mu_R$ around $m_t$.

## Results

As shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:spectrum}, the correction remains
below 2% for $p_T > 200,\mathrm{GeV}$.
The prediction agrees with the measurement within uncertainties.

\begin{figure}
\includegraphics{spectrum.pdf}
\caption{Transverse-momentum distribution at NNLO.}
\label{fig:spectrum}
\end{figure}

For $m_t = 173.5,\mathrm{GeV}$, the distribution depends on $q_T$.
The dependence on $q_T$ is mild. We also study $q_T$ in the tail.
The $p_\mathrm{T}$ distribution is reported in \ref{fig:missing}.

## Definitions

We define $q_T$ as the recoil transverse momentum.

## Conclusion

This proves that the approximation is valid throughout the
entire kinematic region.
